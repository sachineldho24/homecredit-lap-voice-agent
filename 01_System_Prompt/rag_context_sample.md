Offer: pre-approved LAP for existing customers, up to 75 lakh rupees, subject to eligibility check and document verification.
Eligible property types: residential, commercial, industrial. Agricultural property is not eligible.
Repayment tenure: 3 to 15 years.
Documents the senior loan expert will ask for: original property title documents, PAN card, Aadhaar card, last 6 months bank statements, and either the last 3 salary slips (salaried) or the last 2 years ITR (self-employed).
Process after this call: a senior loan expert calls back, shares the exact interest rate and charges, collects documents, and arranges a property valuation and verification.
Interest rates, processing fees and EMI figures are shared only by the senior loan expert. Do not quote them on this call.
For loans already running on the property or EMI reduction, a separate loan transfer specialist handles the case.
